# Privacy

Orbitkeep may record how each Play session went so we can balance the game.

## What we collect when you opt in

- An anonymous install id created on your device
- Run outcome: planet, win or defeat, wave, duration, score, build version
- Fleet and combat choices: loadout, satellite purchases by type and orbit ring, specialization branches, sells, satellite losses
- Draft upgrades offered and picked
- Economy signals: extractor dust, peak and ending stardust, crystals
- Station tech owned: HULL, MAGNET, CACHE
- Earth hull remaining, enemy kinds destroyed, and whether a tactical item was used

## What we do not collect

- Your name or email
- Advertising identifiers
- Precise location
- Screen recordings or session replay

## Choice

Telemetry is off until you accept the in-game prompt. You can turn it off later in Options, Privacy. Turning it off stops new run reports.

## Use

We use this data to find overpowered satellites, weak planets, and economy spikes. We do not use it for ads.

## Google Play Games (Android)

On Android, Orbitkeep can show a global ranking through Google Play Games. This is separate from telemetry and works only if you are signed in to Google Play Games.

- When a run counts toward your records, its final score is sent to Google Play Games, together with your Play Games player profile, so it can appear on the global ranking.
- The ranking shows your Play Games name and avatar to other players. Google handles this data under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- Orbitkeep does not receive your email and does not store your Play Games profile.
- If you never sign in to Google Play Games, no score leaves your device for the ranking.

You can manage or delete your Play Games profile and its data in the Google Play Games settings on your device.

## Deleting your data

Orbitkeep does not have user accounts. The only data tied to you is the anonymous install id described above, and only if you opted in to telemetry.

To ask us to delete the data linked to your install:

1. In the game, open Options, Privacy and turn telemetry off. This stops new run reports.
2. Send an email to gbenatti@gmail.com with the subject "Orbitkeep data deletion". Tell us your Orbitkeep version, your device model and roughly when you played.
3. The install id is stored only on your device and is not shown in the app, so we match reports using those details. We delete every run report we can reasonably match to your install and reply to confirm.

Uninstalling Orbitkeep removes the install id from your device. What we delete: run outcomes, fleet and combat choices, draft picks, economy signals and station tech linked to the install. We keep no other personal data.

## Contact

For access or deletion requests, write to gbenatti@gmail.com (the same address listed on the Orbitkeep Google Play page).
